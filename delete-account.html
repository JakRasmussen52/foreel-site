Delete your account or your data

You can delete everything yourself, in the game, at any time.

Accounts are optional and are not offered in every version of the game. If you never signed in, all your data is on your device and "Delete everything" below removes it.

In the game

Delete your account

Menu > Account > Delete my account. This removes your account and takes your name off every online board. Your progress stays on the phone and you keep playing as a guest.

Delete everything

Menu > Legal & Support > Delete all my data. This deletes your account, if you have one, and erases your catches, coins, gear, progress, settings and saved catch cards from the phone.

If you signed in with a Unity account (email or Google)

Deleting your account in the game removes everything Foreel holds. The Unity account you signed in with is run by Unity and can be used for other games too; to delete that login itself, use Unity's account deletion page.

If you used Sign in with Apple

After deleting your account in the game you can also remove Foreel from your Apple account: on iPhone open Settings > your name > Sign-In & Security > Sign in with Apple, pick Foreel Fishing and tap Stop Using.

If you can't open the game

Email support@foreel.com.au from the address your account uses, or tell us the sign-in method and profile name, and ask for your account to be deleted. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account, email address, profile name, player id, your entries on leaderboards and record boards, and any backup of your game saved with the account.
  • Kept: nothing that identifies you. Records of purchases are held by Apple or Google under their own policies, not by us.

Deleting cannot be undone.
