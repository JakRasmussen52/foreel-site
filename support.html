Support

Email support@foreel.com.au and we'll get back to you.

It helps if you tell us your phone model, the game version (Menu > Legal & Support, top line) and what you were doing when the problem happened. In the game, Menu > Legal & Support > Send error report fills most of that in for you.

Common questions

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

On the old phone open Menu > Legal & Support > Back up & restore and tap Copy backup code. Send the code to yourself. On the new phone copy the code, open the same page and tap Restore from copied code. Restoring replaces whatever is on the new phone. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) also carries your progress to a new phone. Deleting the game without a backup or a backup code loses it.

Do I need to be online?

No. Fishing, saving and rewards all work offline. Online features, when a version of the game offers them, need a connection.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest for as long as you like. The current version has no sign-in; if a later version adds optional accounts, an account will put your profile name on online boards.

How do I delete my data or my account?

See Delete your account.

Is the game for children?

Foreel Fishing is made for players aged 13 and over. If a player says they are under 13 on the first-run screen, the game runs in a young-player mode with no account, no online boards, no statistics, no reminders, no sharing and no links out of the game.
