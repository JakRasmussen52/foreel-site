About the game

Foreel Fishing is a hands-on fishing game: flick to cast, watch for the bite, strike at the right moment, then fight every fish with line tension and drag until it is in the net. It has 14 waters based on real places, from a Florida pier and a New Zealand snapper coast to a Canadian lake, an Amazon river, a Norwegian fjord and the Kona big-game grounds, and more than 100 real species, each with its own habitat, feeding times, baits and fighting style.

Features

  • One-thumb casting and reeling, with optional motion casting and a thumb-crank reel
  • Real tackle choices: rods, reels, line class, leaders, knots, hooks, lures and live bait
  • Methods that match the water: trolling a lure spread for marlin, knife jigging, poppers for giant trevally, squid jigs, berley trails and balloon-rigged live bait
  • Tide, water temperature, time of day and weather change what bites
  • A record book kept by line class, weekly solo tournaments, regional quests and a 3D trophy wall
  • Plays offline; metric and imperial side by side; captions, larger text, reduced motion and colour-blind options
