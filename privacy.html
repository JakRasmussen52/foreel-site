Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026. Applies to the Foreel Fishing game and to this website.

The short version: you can play without an account, the game has no advertising trackers, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Foreel Fishing is published by Foreel, Australia. We are responsible for (the "controller" of) the personal information described here. Contact: support@foreel.com.au.

What stays on your device

Your game is saved on your phone or tablet: level, coins, gear, catches, records, settings, your choices on the first-run screen, and a short log of game errors. We cannot see any of it unless you choose to send it to us.

  • Gameplay statistics. Only if you are 16 or over and tap Allow statistics. The game then sends gameplay events (for example casts, catches, levels, tutorial steps, items bought with in-game coins and error reports) to Unity Gaming Services Analytics, together with a random install id Unity creates, your device model and operating system. Unity works out your approximate country from your IP address. No name, email, account, advertising id or typed text is sent. Turn statistics off at any time (Menu > Legal & Support > Change my choices): the game stops sending and asks Unity to delete what it holds. Never sent for players under 16.
  • Catch cards. When you tap Share, the game makes a picture of your catch. It is saved on your device and handed to the app you pick. On Android a copy is saved to your Pictures folder.
  • Backups. Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include the game's save, under your Apple or Google account.

If you create an account

Accounts and online boards are not available in every version of the game. This section applies only when they are and only if you sign in.

An account is optional. You can sign in with Apple or Game Center (iPhone and iPad), or with a Unity account, which you create with an email address or your Google account on Unity's own sign-in page. If you sign in, we hold:

WhatWhy
A player idTo tell accounts apart
Which sign-in method you used, and the id that method gives usTo sign you in again
Your email address, if you signed up with one or your sign-in provider shares itTo sign you in and reset your password. Never shown to other players
Your profile nameShown beside your catches on leaderboards and record boards

Other players can see your profile name and your board entries. Choose a name that isn't your real one if you prefer. We don't collect your real name, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

What we receive when you contact us

If you email support from the game, the message you send includes your device model, operating system version, game version and, if you choose Send error report, the error log. You see all of it before you send it.

Who else handles your data

  • Unity Technologies processes gameplay statistics for us (Unity Gaming Services Analytics, only with your consent) and, in versions that offer them, runs accounts and leaderboards, storing that data on our behalf. Unity does not use it for its own purposes or for advertising.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them, deliver the game, and provide us with anonymous crash and usage reports if you have agreed to share those in your device settings.

Data may be stored and processed outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

Update and news check

When the game starts with a connection it downloads a small settings file from https://foreel.com.au/config.json, so we can tell you about updates, maintenance and news. This happens for every player, including young-player mode. Like any web request, the website host (GitHub) sees your IP address and device type when this happens and may keep them in its own short-lived server logs under its privacy policy; we do not receive or keep them. Nothing about you or your game is sent.

Notifications

Reminders are created on your device, only if you allow them, and never between 9pm and 9am. You can turn them off in the game or in your device settings.

Children

Foreel Fishing is designed for players 13 and over. If a player tells us they are under 13, the game runs in a restricted mode: no account or sign-in, no online boards, no gameplay statistics, no reminders, no sharing and no links out of the game, and a generated profile name is used. The only network request in that mode is the anonymous update and news check described above, which carries no personal or game data. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe we have, email support@foreel.com.au and we will delete it.

Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide what you ask for (accounts, boards, backups, support replies): performing our agreement with you.
  • Gameplay statistics and reminders: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the game (Menu > Legal & Support > Change my choices) or in your device settings.
  • Keeping boards fair and fixing errors: our legitimate interest in running a working, fair game.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Accounts unused for three years may be deleted. Support emails are kept for up to two years.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your profile name: Menu > Account.
  • Delete your account or all your data: see Delete your account.
  • Change your first-run choices: Menu > Legal & Support > Change my choices.
  • Ask for a copy of the data we hold (including in a portable format), for a correction, or for us to restrict or stop using it: email support@foreel.com.au.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights, including under the Australian Privacy Act, the GDPR and UK GDPR, and United States state privacy laws, such as objecting to processing or complaining to your local privacy regulator. We answer requests within 30 days.

Complaints. Email us first and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au), or, in the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

United States. The categories we may collect are identifiers (player id, email address if you sign up with one) and gameplay data you choose to put online. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising. Send requests to support@foreel.com.au.

Security

Account data travels over encrypted connections and passwords are never stored in readable form. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and for significant changes tell you in the game before they take effect.
