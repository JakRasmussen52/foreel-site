Privacy policy

Effective 21 November 2026. Applies to the Foreel Fishing game and to this website.

The short version: you can play without an account, the game has no advertising trackers, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Foreel Fishing is published by Foreel, Australia. Contact: support@foreel.com.au.

What stays on your device

Your game is saved on your phone or tablet: level, coins, gear, catches, records, settings, your choices on the first-run screen, and a short log of game errors. We cannot see any of it unless you choose to send it to us.

  • Gameplay statistics. If you are 16 or over and switch them on, the game keeps a short history of events (casts, catches, purchases with coins) on your device. They are not uploaded.
  • Catch cards. When you tap Share, the game makes a picture of your catch. It is saved on your device and handed to the app you pick. On Android a copy is saved to your Pictures folder.
  • Backups. Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include the game's save, under your Apple or Google account.

If you create an account

An account is optional. You can sign in with Apple or Game Center (iPhone and iPad), or with a Unity account, which you create with an email address or your Google account on Unity's own sign-in page. If you sign in, we hold:

WhatWhy
A player idTo tell accounts apart
Which sign-in method you used, and the id that method gives usTo sign you in again
Your email address, if you signed up with one or your sign-in provider shares itTo sign you in and reset your password. Never shown to other players
Your profile nameShown beside your catches on leaderboards and record boards
Catches good enough for a board: species, weight, length, tackle, water, fight time, dateTo run the boards and check entries are genuine
A copy of your saved game, only when you tap Back upSo you can restore your progress on another device

Other players can see your profile name and your board entries. Choose a name that isn't your real one if you prefer. We don't collect your real name, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

What we receive when you contact us

If you email support from the game, the message you send includes your device model, operating system version, game version and, if you choose Send error report, the error log. You see all of it before you send it.

Who else handles your data

  • Unity Technologies runs the account and leaderboard service for us (Unity Gaming Services) and stores account data on our behalf.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them, deliver the game, and provide us with anonymous crash and usage reports if you have agreed to share those in your device settings.

Data may be stored and processed outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

Update and news check

When the game starts with a connection it downloads a small settings file from https://foreel.com.au/config.json, so we can tell you about updates, maintenance and news. Like any web request, the website host (GitHub) sees your IP address and device type when this happens. Nothing about you or your game is sent.

Notifications

Reminders are created on your device, only if you allow them, and never between 9pm and 9am. You can turn them off in the game or in your device settings.

Children

Foreel Fishing is for players aged 13 and over. Accounts are not offered to anyone who tells us they are under 13, and for those players the game switches off statistics, reminders and links out of the game. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Accounts unused for three years may be deleted. Support emails are kept for up to two years.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your profile name: Menu > Account.
  • Delete your account or all your data: see Delete your account.
  • Change your first-run choices: Menu > Legal & Support > Change my choices.
  • Ask for a copy of the data we hold, or for a correction: email support@foreel.com.au.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights, including under the Australian Privacy Act, the GDPR and UK GDPR, and United States state privacy laws, such as objecting to processing or complaining to your local privacy regulator. We answer requests within 30 days.

Security

Account data travels over encrypted connections and passwords are never stored in readable form. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and for significant changes tell you in the game before they take effect.
