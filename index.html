Foreel Fishing

Flick to cast, feel the bite, and fight real fish on waters from around the world.

Foreel Fishing is a free fishing game for iPhone, iPad and Android. Every water is based on a real place, and the fish in it are the ones that really live there, with their own habits, favourite baits and ways of fighting.

What's in the game

  • 14 waters, from a Florida pier and a New Zealand snapper coast to an Amazon river, a Norwegian fjord and the Kona big-game grounds
  • More than 100 real species, and a named fish to track down on every water
  • Real tackle choices: rods, reels, line, hooks, knots, lures and live bait
  • A record book kept by line class, weekly tournaments, quests and a trophy wall
  • Plays offline. No account needed
